## Snippet

AAOI 2025Q4 电话会（2026-02-26）完整逐字稿 —— 40% 毛利率的原始路线图，以及分析师直问激光自供比例被绕开的全过程。

【40% 路线图最详细的一次 · Thompson Lin】"As we said, in order to bring up 800G, we need time to fine-tune the production in the year. That's why this is early stage of volume manufacture 800G. That's why I say by Q2 next year, we believe the overall gross margin will be 35%-38% just for transceiver. By end of next year, we believe we can achieve more than 40% gross margin for all the transceiver by Q4 2027." 更精确版本："I will say that, okay, as we say, I think by Q2 or the monthly revenue, sometime in Q2, like June, July, or something like that, the revenue is like $378 million. It depends on the portion of 1.6T transceiver. At the time, I believe the gross margin should be 35%-38% overall gross margin for all the transceiver revenue. I believe we can achieve 40% gross margin by late Q3 or Q4 next year." CFO 当场纠正时间基准："By the way, Simon, just to make sure you're on the same page, the revenue figures that Thompson mentioned were 2027 Q2." / "Not this year." Thompson："Yes, yes... It's very important, okay? No misleading." —— 这里也是 $378M/月 的原始出处，其中 1.6T 约 $71M/月；Q1 上调到 $164M/月，即 $378M→$471M 的约 $93M 增量几乎全部来自 1.6T 单项翻倍以上。

【分析师直问激光自供比例 · 被绕开】Simon Matthew Leopold(Raymond James) 问："I wanted to really focus on the trajectory for gross margin improvement. I want to maybe first start with understanding how much of your laser production is in-house today versus external merchant lasers. And I guess I appreciate that typically, as you ramp production, there is sort of a learning curve of improving yields and things like that. So I would like to make sure we have a good understanding of really the timeline or trajectory to achieve that target you mentioned of 40%. So sort of where are we now, and what is the roadmap?" —— Thompson Lin 的回答【完全跳过当期自供比例】，直接跳到毛利率时间表。唯一量化自供口径出现在同场后段回答另一问题时顺带给出："And, Simon, let you know by end of this year, I would say more than 95% of laser will be AOI laser. Because right now, there is a huge issue of laser shortage. And, actually, even some supplier to us want to get this from them. We—we had at least one year, even longer. So that is why we announced we will invest $300,000,000 in Texas—triple, even more than triple. The purpose, as we said, we need to increase our laser capacity by Q2 next year, and that is to fulfill our transceiver demand."（注：两个独立转录源均把 AOI 误转为 AI，为该系列逐字稿的固定转录缺陷，可交叉确认为同一句。）—— 结论：EML 单品自供率、InP 衬底自制率【从未披露】；能引用的最强口径是"到 2026 年底 >95% 激光器为自产"，且这是 2026-02 的前瞻说法。
