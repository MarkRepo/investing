## 核心数据点与事实

- [Investing.com 电话会逐字稿][2026Q2][管理层原话] 「We recorded non-GAAP gross margin of **29.8%**, which was **in line with our guidance range of 29%-30%**.」-> 本批唯一给出 **Q2 事前毛利率指引区间**的来源，用于判定「超预期」的真实成色。
- [同上][管理层原话] 「Our **non-GAAP income per share of $0.06** was **above our guidance range of a loss of $0.03 to earnings of $0.03**.」-> Q2 非 GAAP EPS 实际 $0.06；事前指引 -$0.03 至 +$0.03。
- [同上][管理层原话] 收入 $191.9M「**in line with our guidance range of $180 million-$198 million**」。
- [推算][「超预期」拆解] 三项对比事前指引：收入 = 区间内（中值 $189M，实际 $191.9M，仅超中值 1.5%）、毛利率 = 区间内、**只有 EPS 超区间上限**（$0.06 vs 上限 $0.03）。-> 所谓「tops Q2 2026 forecasts」**主要是 EPS 超预期，收入与毛利率均只是达标**；股价 +20.5%（mat-0ab2e2）建立在一个中值仅超 1.5% 的收入之上。
- [同上][管理层原话][K1] 「to support next generation AI infrastructure remains so robust that our **near-term revenue is bounded almost entirely by production capacity and key component availability**.」并「continue to anticipate **steady sequential revenue growth** this year and continue non-GAAP profitability」。
- [推算][K1 关键矛盾] 管理层原话只承诺「**steady sequential growth**（平稳的环比增长）」，而 FY26 约 $1.1B 隐含 Q4 需 **QoQ +71%**（$272.5M -> 约 $484M）。**「steady sequential」与「+71% QoQ」在语义上互斥** -> 或 FY26 $1.1B 将下修，或「steady」一词被极度宽松使用。这是本批最尖锐的内部不一致。
- [同上][管理层原话][K4] 第一优先事项表述为「scaling our next generation data center products, including both our **400G and 800G** solutions, by expanding our production capacity **in a disciplined manner**」。-> 管理层自述的优先级里**没有 1.6T**，且用了「disciplined（有节制的）」限定产能扩张。
- [同上][管理层原话] 第二优先事项为「**diversifying our revenue base**」（客户/收入基础多元化）—— 对应 brief K6，管理层已把集中度当作需主动解决的问题。

## 叙事主线
因为管理层的原话只承诺「平稳的环比增长」、把在建品类说成「400G 和 800G」、把扩产限定为「有节制」-> 所以口头指引的实际温度**远低于** FY26「约 $1.1B」所隐含的 Q4 +71% 台阶，也低于 brief 里管理层 mid-2027 年化 $5.65B 的激进口径。对投资意味着：thesis 的核心分歧（管理层 $5.65B vs 一致预期 $2.5B）在本份逐字稿里找不到管理层侧的支撑语言 —— 至少在 2026Q2 的正式发言稿部分，管理层的措辞是保守的。

## 反常识/分歧点
- 市场预期/常识：AAOI Q2「大幅超预期」，故股价 +20.5% 合理。本文表明：**收入和毛利率都只是落在自家指引区间内**，仅 EPS 超上限 $0.03 -> 股价反应远大于基本面超预期幅度，估值端（P/S 16.1x）承担了绝大部分涨幅。
- 市场预期/常识：产能扩张是「不惜代价抢份额」。本文表明：管理层用 **「in a disciplined manner」** 描述扩产 —— 与 mat-181ed0 提示的 2026H2 资本开支强度上升、以及 $600M ATM 并存时，「disciplined」更像是对稀释质疑的应答话术，需在后续季度用实际 capex 验证。
- **K3 关联**：管理层在正式发言中主动提「diversifying our revenue base」与「disciplined」扩产，却（在本片段中）未提任何融资安排 -> ATM/权证的稀释成本在电话会叙事中被淡化。

## 全文新增数据点

### ① mid-2027 $471M/月 —— Q2 只是重申，出处在 Q1
- [CFO Stefan Murry @0:18:33][管理层原话] "Even with this temporary weakness in 100G, **we continue to believe** that by mid-2027, 100G and 400G revenue will be approximately $90 million, 800G revenue will be approximately $217 million, and 1.6 terabit revenue will be approximately $164 million monthly. In total, this is about **$471 million per month** of data center transceiver revenue."
  → 措辞是 **continue to believe**（重申），首次提出在 Q1 电话会（2026-05-07，见 mat-4e8a59）。**因此"Q2 电话会提出 $471M"的说法不准确。**
- [同上] Q3 100G 下滑原因原话："one of our customers' inability to source sufficient 100G switches to meet their initial forecast. We believe that this shortage of switches is related to **memory shortage**" → 100G 弱是**客户侧交换机缺内存**导致，非 AAOI 自身问题，但会压 Q3 产品结构。
- [CEO Thompson Lin @0:32:08 / @0:33:13] "the overall transceiver revenue will increase from today's number to $471 million per month. That's huge growth." / "All growth, actually, from 800G and 1.6T, 100G will go down... Most of the increased capacity will be in U.S. Even so... that is not good enough for the customer demand. **The customer demand is 20%-40% higher.**"

### ② 40% 毛利率 —— 长期目标无时间表，且年底出口值被下修到 32%-33%
- [CFO][管理层原话] "**We remain committed to our long-term objective of returning non-GAAP gross margins to around 40%**... as our mix shifts toward higher margin products and as we capture additional efficiencies" —— **通篇不给时间表**（对比 Q4'25 曾明确说 "40% by late Q3 or Q4 2027"，见 mat-89a180）。且重申 "the revenue mix in data center in the short term will be **a slight headwind**"。
- [CEO Thompson Lin @0:49:12][**关键：分析师问 mid-30s，CEO 答 32%-33%**] Michael Genovese(Rosenblatt) 问 "do we still expect to **exit the year in the mid-30s** of gross margin?" → Thompson Lin："The gross margin for sure will still be better, because we need to pay some expertise fee for some key supplier, **I would say, 32%-33%.**"
  → **年底出口毛利率口径由 mid-30s 下修到 32%-33%**，且下修理由是"要付关键供应商的 expertise fee"（成本项，非结构改善）。
- [同上] 驱动项**全部押在产品结构**："The most important is how much is the 1.6T, because that's **highest gross margin product**... For sure by Q3, Q4 next year, when we deliver the CPO laser or EOSAP module... **gross margin for laser is about 55%-65%. For EOSAP... more than 50%**, that's more like Q3, Q4 next year."
  → **管理层未给良率改善或自供比例提升的任何量化贡献**——40% 的路径完全依赖"1.6T 与 laser/EOSAP 占比上升"这一 mix 论证。
- [同上] Q2 OpEx 超支原话："higher shipping costs associated with the rapid ramp in **CATV** revenue in the quarter, combined with higher than expected R&D spending, as we have been asked by customers to **qualify new 800G and 1.6T products**"

### ③ FY2026 非 GAAP 营业利润指引被静默撤下（本轮最有价值的"缺席证据"）
- [Q2 电话会指引段 + Q2 官方新闻稿] **双双不含任何 FY2026 非 GAAP 营业利润/利润率数字**，只留 "Looking more broadly at 2026, we believe our 2026 revenue will be **around $1.1 billion**"。
- 三代口径演变：Q4'25「>$1B 营收 + **>$120M** 非 GAAP 营业利润」→ Q1'26「**exceed** $1.1B + **>$140M**」→ Q2'26「**around** $1.1B + **无营业利润指引**」。
  → 营收口径由 exceed 降为 around；营业利润指引直接消失。
- 算术旁证（为何必然撤下）：Q2 实际非 GAAP 营业**亏损 $10.3M**、H1 累计仍为营业亏损；Q3 指引隐含营业利润中值仅约 $4-8M（GP $74-88M − OpEx $70-80M）→ FY >$140M 已不可达。
  → **市场上仍在引用的 "FY26 非 GAAP 营业利润 >$120M" 是两代前（2026-02，配 $1B 营收基数）的存量数字。**

### ④ 1.6T 认证进度（K4 定案）
- [CFO @0:10:19] "We expect **full qualification** of our first 1.6 terabit product by this customer **within the next couple of weeks**, followed by shipments of 1.6 terabit beginning **later this quarter**. We continue to expect that shipments of these orders will return this customer as a **10% plus customer** for us in Q3."
- [CEO @0:43:39][**供应商序位**] "**AOI will be the fourth supplier qualified by one big hyperscale data center customer for 1.6T transceiver.**... it should be finished within maybe, I would say, **two, three weeks**. We can start to deliver by end of this quarter. Right now **we have more than $200 million orders in hand**... our target is to finish all the orders by sometime in Q2 [2027]."
  → **K4 判定：截至 Q2'26 仍是 0 家完成量产认证、1 家在途，且 AAOI 是该客户的第 4 家供应商**（不是独家、不是首选）。
- [同上] Q4 可交付量与**约束性质**："we can deliver more than $70 million of revenue in Q4 for 1.6 terabit... right now what we can commit, maybe **$70 million-$80 million in Q4. Not because of capacity, because, I would say, material constraint.**" → **CEO 亲口说 Q4 的约束是"材料"而非产能**，这与全案"产能受限"叙事口径不同。
- [同上] 2027 展望："based on the customer feedback, the volume we're talking about is **more than 500,000 transceivers per month by end of next year**... I would say **$300 million-$350 million** [per month]"；Q1'27 "will be **double** Q4 or more than double"。
- [CFO 对客户广度的谨慎] "Until we have sufficient capacity to service multiple customers, **we have to be careful about taking too many orders**... we definitely don't want to overpromise what we can actually deliver. We're being appropriately circumspect."

### ⑥ 产能口径（K5 定案）与名义 vs 可发货定义
- [CFO 准备发言][**口径确认**] "our total manufacturing capacity is **approaching 200,000 units per month**, up from nearly 100,000 units per month **of 800G and 1.6 terabit capacity** at the end of Q1... by the end of this year... **over 650,000 pieces of 800G and 1.6 terabit products per month**... ### ⑦ Q4 桥 —— 管理层三个口径互不自洽（本份最尖锐的发现之一）
- (a) [CFO 确认分析师算术] Simon Leopold："some quick arithmetic suggests that in the fourth quarter, you're anticipating the combination of **800G and 1.6T revenue in the neighborhood of $330 million**" → Stefan Murry："**Yeah, that's about right, directionally.**"
- (b) [CEO @0:46:45] "In Q4... we should be able to deliver, I would say, **more than $500 million revenue**... It's like **60% of growth from Q3 to Q4**."
- (c) [CFO @0:26:40] FY "**around $1.1 billion**"。

- **最陡的一步**：若 Q4 800G+1.6T ≈ $330M 成立，而 Q3 800G 约 $62M（$12.8M × nearly 5x）、1.6T Q4 上限 $80M → **800G 需在 Q4 单季从约 $62M 跳到约 $250M（约 4 倍）**。且 CEO 亲口说 Q4 的约束是 **material constraint** 而非产能。
- [Q3 口径 · CEO @0:32:08] "compared to Q2... we're talking about **40% growth**... 35%-45% growth. The growth is mainly from 800G... because of memory issue for the switch, **100G will decrease by $20 million-$25 million**. Otherwise, our Q3 revenue, the growth should be more than 50%."

### ⑧ CPO / ELSFP —— 自用优先，时间表已后移
- [CFO] "When we've talked to several of the major CPO customers, **they love our laser. We just can't make enough of them to be involved in their current first-generation deployments**... **We have to prioritize our ability to make lasers for our own transceivers first**, and then as we expand the fab... then we'll have more capacity for the ESAP, for the CPO type laser."
  → **AAOI 已错过 CPO 第一代部署**，原因是自有激光产能要先保自家模块。
- [CEO] "the overall [laser] capacity will increase by **almost 300% by Q3 next year. That's not enough.** That's why we are building a clean room of the **second fab** in Houston area. The size will be about **four times** of the current facility."
