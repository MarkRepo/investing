## 核心数据点与事实

- [AAOI 官方新闻稿标题原文] "Applied Optoelectronics Announces Definitive Agreement to **Sell its Chinese Manufacturing Facilities and Certain Assets Related to its Transceiver Business for $150 Million**"
- [创始人/董事长/CEO **Dr. Thompson Lin** 原话] "After careful consideration, we concluded that it is in the best interest of the company and our shareholders for **AOI to exit the transceiver market and focus our resources on our CATV business and manufacturing lasers and laser components** for the […]"
- [同上] "The significant proceeds from this transaction will enable us to make strategic investments in **higher margin and higher growth opportunities**."
- [CFO 兼首席战略官 **Dr. Stefan Murry** 原话] "This transaction is part of our plan to **accelerate our path to profitability**. We intend to use the majority of the proceeds from the sale for increased investments in the business, **particularly in our fast-growing CATV business and some of our newer laser-related products**."
- [Use of Proceeds 原文] 交易交割前，AOI 拟以**相当于预估对价 4%-10%** 的金额**反投买方、换取买方 10% 股权**；余款用于一般营运资金。

## 对投资的意味（相对 case 现有表述的增量）

1. **一手化**：case 环①梁二与镜鉴 2 的核心论断（"管理层曾判定光模块盈利空间越来越小并宣布退出"）此前靠二手中文报道；现在有**公司自己的新闻稿 + CEO/CFO 双人原话**。论断本身成立且更硬。
2. **动因措辞比 case 引用的更强**：官方口径不是含糊的"盈利空间小"，而是明确的 **"exit the transceiver market"** + **"higher margin and higher growth opportunities"** + **"accelerate our path to profitability"**——即当年管理层把**光模块定性为低毛利、拖累盈利路径的业务**，并把 **CATV 与激光器定性为高毛利高增长方向**。这与今天的结构（模块 GM 27.7% vs 激光 55-65%）指向同一判断。
3. **新增细节（case 无）**：AOI 当时准备**反投买方拿 10% 股权**——说明这不是单纯甩卖，而是"退出制造+保留上游与分成"的结构设计。
4. **对反方论据的公平记录**：反方主张"2022 年 GM 15% 时判断光模块低毛利是对的、且交易是对手方终止而非管理层反悔"——本份新闻稿只证明**当年的判断内容与动因**，不能证明谁终止；终止过程仍以 mat-d9c8af（2023-09 突发终止、2024-01-02 仕佳光子公告终止入股）为源。
