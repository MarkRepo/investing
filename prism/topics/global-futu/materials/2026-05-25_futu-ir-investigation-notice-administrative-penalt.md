# Futu IR — Investigation Notice & Administrative Penalty Pre-Notification (2026-05-22)

**URL**: https://ir.futuholdings.com/node/9166/pdf

## Snippet

Futu Holdings 公告收到 CSRC 调查通知及行政处罚预先告知函；拟罚款 RMB 1.85B(~USD271M)；CEO 李华个人罚款 RMB 1.25M；违反《证券法》《证券投资基金法》《期货及衍生品法》三法；尚待听证及最终决定。

## Full text (via WebFetch)

Proposed Penalties:
- Total fine: approximately RMB1.85 billion (approximately USD271 million)
- CEO personal fine: RMB1.25 million (~USD 183,575) for founder Li Hua

Alleged Violations: Companies violated three Chinese laws — Securities Law, Securities Investment Fund Law, Futures and Derivatives Law — by conducting unlicensed securities business, public fund sales business and futures business.

Entities Involved: Certain Futu entities in mainland China and Hong Kong engaged in prohibited activities.

Rectification Status: Maintained ongoing communication with CSRC and implemented rectification measures.

Mainland Account Exposure: Funded accounts from mainland China accounted for approximately 13% of the Company's total funded accounts as of Q1 2026.

Rights & Procedural Status: Entitled to submit statements, present defenses, and request a hearing. Proposed penalty remains subject to further proceedings and the final determination by the CSRC.
