# Tesla’s ‘unsupervised’ Robotaxis vanish a week after pre-earnings announcement

Avatar for Fred Lambert  Fred Lambert | Jan 28 2026 - 10:40 am PT

73 Comments

Elon Musk Tesla robotaxi 

A week ago, Elon Musk announced that Tesla had started Robotaxi rides in Austin “with no safety monitor in the car.” The stock jumped 4%. Headlines celebrated the milestone. But since then, not a single rider has reported actually getting one of these unsupervised rides. [...] View all comments

As we reported last week, the “unsupervised” Robotaxis spotted on January 22 were all being followed by trailing Tesla vehicles with safety monitors inside. Tesla didn’t remove supervision, it just moved it to a different car.

Now even that setup appears to have been scaled back or paused entirely. The vast majority of rides have safety monitors back inside the vehicle, sitting in either the driver’s seat or passenger seat. [...] David Moss, a Tesla enthusiast who gained notoriety for driving 10,000 miles on FSD v14 without interventions, traveled to Austin specifically to find and ride in an unsupervised Robotaxi. He’s been hunting for days.
