# Anti-Obesity Medication: Current Market & Emerging Trends

## Current FDA-Approved Medications (2023)
The U.S. market features six primary options:
- Semaglutide (Wegovy): GLP-1 receptor agonist with 12% placebo-adjusted weight loss
- Tirzepatide (Zepbound): Dual GLP-1/GIP agonist achieving 18.4% weight reduction
- Liraglutide (Saxenda): GLP-1 agonist with 4% effectiveness
- Phentermine/topiramate (Qsymia): Amphetamine combination for short-term use
- Naltrexone/bupropion (Contrave): 5% weight loss via hypothalamus pathway
- Orlistat (Xenical): Lipase inhibitor with modest 3kg reduction

## GLP-1 Dominance & Market Shift
GLP-1 receptor agonists transformed obesity treatment in the early 2020s, becoming more effective than earlier drugs and causing shortages for diabetes patients.

## 2025-2026 Regulatory Changes
Oral formulations entered the market:
- December 2025: FDA approved oral semaglutide
- April 2026: Orforglipron (Foundayo) approval as first flexible-timing oral GLP-1

## Emerging Pipeline Candidates
Advanced-stage compounds show promise:
- Retatrutide: Triple agonist (GLP-1/GIP/glucagon) achieving 24% weight loss
- Cagrilintide/semaglutide: Combined therapy reaching 20.4% reduction
- VK2735 & Survodutide: Dual agonists in Phase 3 trials

## Critical Limitation: Weight Regain
A significant challenge persists: After stopping treatment with GLP-1 agonists...people regain on average more than half (50-70%) of the lost weight within 1 year. Long-term medication adherence remains necessary for sustained results.
