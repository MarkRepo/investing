# Tirzepatide: Recent Clinical and Regulatory Developments

## Phase 3 Trial Data & Weight Loss Efficacy
The SURMOUNT trials demonstrated substantial weight reduction across dosages. At 72 weeks, tirzepatide produced mean weight decreases of -15.0% (5mg), -19.5% (10mg), and -20.9% (15mg), compared to -3.1% for placebo.

## Zepbound New Indications
Beyond weight management, Zepbound recently gained expanded approval. In December 2024, the FDA authorized its use for moderate to severe obstructive sleep apnea in adults with obesity, marking the first medication approved for this condition.

## 2024-2025 Clinical Advances
A landmark finding emerged from the SURMOUNT-1 three-year study: tirzepatide reduced the risk of developing type 2 diabetes by 94% in adults with pre-diabetes and obesity or overweight.

Cardiovascular benefits were also documented, with tirzepatide decreasing the risk of major cardiovascular complications by 38% compared to a placebo in heart failure patients.

## Market Performance
In 2023, tirzepatide achieved significant uptake, ranking as the 110th-most commonly prescribed medication in the U.S., with more than six million prescriptions.

## 2025-2026 Policy Developments
The Trump Administration declined to mandate GLP-1 coverage through Medicare/Medicaid but introduced TrumpRx, offering tirzepatide at reduced prices ($50/month for Medicare patients opting in).
