# Orforglipron Overview

What is it?
Orforglipron (brand name Foundayo) is an oral GLP-1 receptor agonist developed by Eli Lilly for weight management. The compound was originally discovered by Chugai Pharmaceutical and licensed to Lilly in 2018.

FDA Approval
The medication received U.S. approval in April 2026, marketed as the only GLP-1 pill for weight loss that can be taken any time of day without food or water restrictions.

How it works
As a small-molecule partial GLP-1 agonist, orforglipron affects cyclic adenosine monophosphate activity, mimicking glucagon-like peptide-1's effects on appetite suppression and blood glucose regulation.

Clinical Trial Results (ACHIEVE-1)
In the Phase III trial with 559 type 2 diabetes patients over 40 weeks:
- Hemoglobin A1C reduction: 1.3-1.6 percentage points from 8% baseline
- Weight loss at highest dose: approximately 8% body weight (16 lbs/7.3 kg average)
- Over 65% of high-dose participants achieved non-diabetic A1C levels

Side Effects
Most common adverse events include gastrointestinal discomfort (44-70%), diarrhea, nausea, upset stomach, and constipation—generally mild to moderate in severity.
