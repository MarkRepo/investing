# Mazdutide: Key Information

What it is: Mazdutide (also called IBI362 or LY3305677) is a dual agonist targeting the GLP-1 receptor and glucagon receptor, based on oxyntomodulin.

Developers: Eli Lilly and Innovent Biologics created this pharmaceutical compound together.

Clinical Status: The drug progressed through Phase III clinical trials. Once-weekly mazdutide led to clinically relevant reductions in body weight in Chinese adults with obesity or being overweight, according to May 2025 trial results published in New England Journal of Medicine.

Regulatory Approval: In June 2025, China's regulatory authority approved mazdutide injection for marketing.

Administration: The medication is delivered by injection on a once-weekly schedule.

Mechanism: As a poly-agonist, mazdutide activates multiple metabolic pathways simultaneously, distinguishing it from single-target diabetes and obesity medications.

The compound represents advancement in the GLP-1 poly-agonist drug category, joining other multi-receptor agents in treating metabolic conditions.
