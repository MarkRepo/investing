# Semaglutide: Key Data and Developments

## Sales Performance
Semaglutide generated the highest medication sales in the US during 2023, with expenditures of $38.6 billion USD. It ranked as the nineteenth most commonly prescribed medication in the United States that year, exceeding 25 million prescriptions.

## Notable Regulatory Updates (2025-2026)
The FDA expanded semaglutide indications to include:
- Treatment for metabolic-associated steatohepatitis (MASH) with moderate-to-advanced liver fibrosis (August 2025)
- Cardiovascular risk reduction for high-risk type 2 diabetes patients via oral formulation (October 2025)
- Oral weight management version approved under Wegovy brand (December 2025)

## Pricing Developments
