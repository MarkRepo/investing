## 核心数据点与事实

- **[Bettles/Q&A][2026-07-30] exit-2026 InP 季度产能 ≈ $60M** —— Savageaux 问"新目标是否约 $60M 季度 InP 产能"，答"Yes, that's about right... About $60 million thereabouts"。年化 **$240M**。
- **[Bettles/Q&A] exit-2027 目标"约 $130M/季"** —— "next year, we're looking at doubling, slightly more than doubling again, take our **revenue** to somewhere in the region of about $130 million a quarter exiting the year"。年化 **$520M**。⚠️ 措辞是 "our revenue"（可能为公司总营收口径），而 $60M 那档明确是 InP 产能；Needham 复述为"$70M→$130M/季"。**环②建模须显式选定口径**。
- **[Shi/Q&A] 原 capex 计划：2026 约 $40M、2027 约 $100M**（分析师模型口径，管理层未否认）。
- **[Bettles/Q&A] 产能上修后 capex 不加** —— "there is actually not a lot of additional CapEx spend here... we can **gain capacity without huge additional CapEx spend**"；并确认"reaffirming the CapEx plan you previously communicated"→"That's correct. That's correct."
- **[Bettles/Q&A] 产能增量来源** —— "part of the capacity increase... isn't just a CapEx spend, but it's a **productivity and a product mix change**"（新长晶炉设计 + 2→3→4 寸迁移）。
- **[Fischer/Q&A] 毛利率管理层目标"5 开头"** —— "We should definitely be **targeting a number that begins with the 5**"；另 "management's target is a number that begins with 4%... push this over 40% and **stay at 45% for now, but put your seatbelts on**"。
- **[Shannon/Q&A] 分析师原话"how close to the asymptote we are"** —— 渐近线问题被当场提出，管理层未给数字上限。
- **[Young/Q&A] InP 线满产 + 产多少卖多少** —— "the whole indium phosphide line is **fully utilized**"；"过去 2 寸小尺寸不受欢迎卖不掉，现在客户什么都要，**whatever we can produce, we can sell**. That also will help us in terms of margins"。
- **[Young/Q&A] 分产品毛利率定性排序** —— "everybody knows our **indium phosphide business has better margin than the other 2 businesses**, the gallium arsenide and JVs"；下季增长全来自 InP → "by simple math, the gross margin is going to be better"。
- **[Young/Q&A] 大尺寸迁移抬毛利率** —— 2→3 寸、3→4 寸，"now there's a big push towards 6-inch"，"This gives us a great opportunity to **increase our gross margins**"。
- **[Bettles/Q&A] backlog >$100M、覆盖到 2027** —— "it exceeds **2 quarters** for sure"；"In terms of 2027, we're **covered with backlog going out into 2027**"；LTSA 覆盖"2027 and beyond"；"doesn't include a lot of the **China business**"（境内不需许可、可更快周转）。
- **[Young/Q&A] 故意不接单** —— "we are **not taking orders**... Right now, it [production queue] is full... if we open up the floodgate it's going to be huge... 不然可以报 **$150M** 背单"。
- **[Young/Q&A] 可见度 3–4 个季度**（此前只有 1–2 个季度）。
- **[Bettles/Q&A] 中国占比** —— "China right now is definitely **above 50%** of our revenue in Q2"，指引"**40% to 60%** range"。
- **[Young/Q&A] 境内无许可** —— "there is **no permit required** to ship our product within China"。
- **[Bettles/Q&A] 长约收入落在后面** —— 新签 LTSA "don't move the needle too much on Q2"，Q3 起变大；**Casella 合同 2027 才开始**（"the Casela deal doesn't start until 2027" → "That's a good point"）；Lumentum 总收入影响不披露。
- **[Bettles/Q&A] 许可趋于规律化** —— "we are seeing **more regularity in the process, especially in certain geographic regions**"；但"**cannot apply for a permit without an order in place**"。
- **[Bettles/Young/Q&A] VCSEL 共存而非替代** —— AXT 已向 2 家大型数据中心/激光公司供 GaAs VCSEL 衬底；"InP lasers, silicon photonics and GaAs-based VCSELs **coexisting**"；200G 以上必须 InP。
- **[Young/Q&A] 自陈定价权来源** —— "indium phosphide, we have **more margins out of the supply chain**, and we are a **dominant player** in indium phosphide"。
- **[缺口确认] 全场 Q&A 无人问、也无人给出分地区或分产品毛利率数字** —— 管理层只给定性排序。

## 叙事主线

因为需求持续超过产能（backlog >$100M 且覆盖到 2027、故意限单、InP 线满产）→ 管理层判断产能可在 **不加 capex** 的前提下从 exit-2026 $60M/季 翻到 exit-2027 约 $130M/季（靠 productivity + 大尺寸迁移），且毛利率目标是"5 开头" → 对投资意味着：**"扩产必须靠巨额 capex、扩产必然自我打崩价格"这条链在管理层口径下不成立**；同时 $632.5M 募资里只有约 22%（$140M）有既定用途，"钱花不出去"的一侧被自证。

## 反常识/分歧点

- **市场/本研究预期**：$6.94 亿现金要么花不出去、要么花出去打崩价格（命门 4 的两难）。**本文表明**：存在第三条路 —— 产能靠 productivity 与大尺寸迁移增长、capex 维持原计划 $140M，现金既不必花、产能也照涨。**命门 4 必须从两难改写为三路。**
- **本研究预期**：44.9% 已在渐近线（41.5%）之上、规模再大也补不上来。**本文表明**：管理层把进一步改善明确挂在 volume + productivity + 大尺寸 mix 上，自设目标"5 开头"；分析师当场问"离渐近线多远"而管理层拒绝设上限。
- **本研究预期（环⑤ R1）**：2027H2 供给集中投放打崩价格。**本文表明**：AXT 自己 2027 已被 backlog + LTSA 覆盖，且被点名的自建替代者 Coherent/Lumentum 是预付款锁货方；但 **Casella/Lumentum 收入 2027 才起** —— 这既是增量确认，也意味着 2026 的高毛利率并非由长约支撑。
- **本研究预期**：中国营收占比 65%（Q2）且指引 40:60 移向低毛利内销。**本文口径**：Q2"above 50%"、指引 40–60% 区间 —— 与 case 的 65% 有口径差（case 用 10-Q 分地区数、管理层用另一口径），需在环①核对。
