# Prohibiting Russian Uranium Imports Act 118th Congress (2023-2024)

**URL**: https://www.congress.gov/bill/118th-congress/house-bill/1042

## Snippet

This bill limits the importation of uranium from Russia. Specifically, it bans unirradiated low-enriched uranium (ie, uranium that has not been in a reactor)
