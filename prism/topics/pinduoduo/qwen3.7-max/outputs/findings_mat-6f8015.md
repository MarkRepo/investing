## 核心数据点与事实

- **Q1 2026 总收入 ¥106.2B ($15.4B)** (+11% YoY, vs Q1 2025 ¥95.7B)
- **Online marketing ¥49.9B** (vs ¥48.7B, +2.5% — 接近停滞)
- **Transaction services ¥56.3B** (+20% YoY, vs ¥47.0B — Temu 驱动)
- **总成本 ¥46.9B** (+15% YoY): fulfillment + bandwidth + payment processing
- **总运营费用 ¥39.8B** (+3% YoY): S&M ¥33.8B (+1%), G&A ¥1.6B (-6%), R&D ¥4.4B (+22%)
- **经营利润 ¥19.6B** (+22% YoY): margin 18.5% (vs Q1 2025 16.8%)
- **Non-GAAP 经营利润 ¥21.1B** (+15%): margin 19.9%
- **净利润 ¥12.5B** (-15% YoY, vs ¥14.7B): NI margin 11.8%
- **Non-GAAP 净利润 ¥14.1B** (-17% YoY, vs ¥16.9B): 远低于共识 ¥24.6B (miss 43%)
- **Non-GAAP diluted EPS ¥9.51** (vs ¥11.41 in Q1 2025)
- **经营现金流 ¥16.4B** (vs ¥15.5B, +6%)
- **现金+短期投资 ¥436.1B ($63.2B)** (vs ¥422.3B at YE2025, +3.3%)
- 管理层引语 — Lei Chen (Co-CEO): "deep transformations in our business, internal processes and organization"
- Jiazhen Zhao (Co-CEO): "supply chain investments will be our core strategic priority... building first-party brand business"
- Jun Liu (VP Finance): "investing resolutely in supply chain capabilities... prepared to invest over the long term"
- USD/RMB 汇率: 6.8980 (2026-03-31)

## 叙事主线

因为 Q1 operating profit +22% 但 net income -15% → 差额来自非经营性损失(投资亏损/汇兑) + "deep transformation"投入 → 对投资意味着经营层仍在改善(+22% OP)，但管理层明确发出"长期投入"信号(供应链/自营品牌)，利润拐点时间进一步不确定

## 反常识/分歧点

- 市场预期：Q1 miss 说明经营恶化 → 实际 OP +22%、S&M 仅 +1%（费用控制良好），miss 来自非经常性项目
- 市场预期：管理层会安抚市场 → 三位高管齐声说"长期投入"(supply chain/first-party brand)，等于告诉市场"利润压力是故意的"
