# Trump Second-Term China Tariff Timeline (Wikipedia 2026-05-28)

## Initial Tariffs (Feb 2025)
- 2025-02-01: IEEPA emergency, 10% on Chinese goods (fentanyl)
- 2025-03-04: raised to 20%

## Liberation Day (Apr 2025)
- 2025-04-02: 10% baseline reciprocal effective 04-05
- Mid-April: China hit 145% (after retaliation), US kept "fentanyl tariff" alongside

## Negotiated Reduction (Jun-Oct 2025)
- 90-day deal (exp 2025-11-09): US tariff on China = 30% (10% baseline + 20% fentanyl)
- China lowered counter-tariffs to 10%, resumed rare-earth exports

## Trump-Xi (Late Oct 2025)
- Fentanyl tariff: 20% → 10%
- Total China tariff: 20%

## Section 301 Status
- Threatened but only enacted vs Nicaragua (10% starting 2027)
- No Section 301 tariffs on China enacted this period

## De Minimis
- Global exemption closed 2025-08-29
- Cross-border e-commerce packages affected
- Enforcement specifics for Temu/Shein undocumented in this source

## Current Status (Mar 2026)
China-specific tariffs substantially lower than April 2025 peaks
