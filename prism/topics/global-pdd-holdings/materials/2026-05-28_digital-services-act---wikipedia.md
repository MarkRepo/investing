# EU DSA Wikipedia (抓取 2026-05-28)

## VLOP Penalty Structure
- Primary fine: up to 6% global annual turnover
- Periodic penalties: up to 5% daily worldwide turnover
- Nuclear option: temporary service suspension for persistent violations

## Enforcement Progress (2025-11)
- 14 investigations targeting VLOP/VLOSE compliance
- Platforms: AliExpress, FB, IG, Temu, TikTok, X, + porn platforms

## First Major Fine: X (2025-12)
- Violation: deceptive design in X Premium blue check; inadequate ad transparency; researcher data access
- Fine: €120M ($140M)
- Breakdown: €45M (deceptive design) + €35M (ad transparency) + €40M (data access)

## Chinese Cross-Border Platforms
- Temu (designated 2024-05): under active investigation, no final penalties yet
- Shein (designated 2024-04): VLOP, investigation ongoing
- TikTok (designated 2023-04): accepted commitments 2024-08 (withdrew TikTok Lite addictive rewards targeting minors); investigation into 2024 Romanian election interference
