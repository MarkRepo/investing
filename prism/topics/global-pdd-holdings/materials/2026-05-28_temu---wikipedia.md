# Temu Business Data Summary (Wikipedia 2026-05-28)

## Market Coverage
As of April 2025, Temu operates in "more than 90 markets." Expanded from Sept 2022 US launch, entering Europe April 2023 and South Africa January 2024.

## User Metrics & Downloads
- US Users: 100 million active users reached by Feb 2024
- Global Downloads: 130M+ app downloads globally (as of Feb 2024)
- Monthly Active Users: By Dec 2024, Temu's MAU surpassed Amazon's
- Monthly Visits: ~700M monthly visits to Temu.com (Dec 2024)

## Business Model Evolution
- Traditional Model: Direct China-to-consumer shipping leveraging $800 US de minimis exemption
- 2024 Shift - Local Seller Program: enables local sellers to sell + fulfill within their regions. As of Oct 2025, operational in 30+ countries (US, UK, France, Italy, Japan, Mexico, Australia)
- Tariff Impact: In May 2025, following Trump's closure of de minimis exception, Temu announced it would "stop selling goods from China directly to US customers," shifting to locally-based fulfillment

## Competitive Positioning
- vs Amazon: "one of Amazon's biggest challenges in years." By Aug 2024, Temu user numbers = 91% of Amazon's
- vs Shein: Ongoing legal disputes (copyright/antitrust), UK trial late 2026

## Regulatory Challenges
- EU DSA: European Commission launched investigations Oct 2024 over illegal products, addictive design, seller reappearance. July 2025: Temu found "breaking EU rules by failing to prevent the sale of illegal products"
- Data Privacy: Multiple class action lawsuits in US states; South Korea + European regulators investigating
- Forced Labor: 2023 US House report found "extremely high risk" of forced labor in supply chains

## Operating Metrics (PDD Holdings Parent)
PDD maintains lean operations with 34% operating margin and ~17,000 employees—outpacing Alibaba (15% margin) and JD.com (3% margin).
