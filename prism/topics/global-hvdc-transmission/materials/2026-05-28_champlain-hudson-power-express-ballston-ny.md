# Champlain Hudson Power Express | Ballston, NY

**URL**: https://www.townofballstonny.gov/1314/Champlain-Hudson-Power-Express

## Snippet

The Champlain Hudson Power Express (CHPE) is an innovative power transmission project that will deliver clean, renewable hydropower to the New York metro area. Developed by Transmission Developers Inc.(TDI), which is owned by Blackstone, Inc, to deliver reliable clean energy from Hydro-Québec in Canada directly to New York City, the transmission line will play a key role in New York's energy transformation. The CHPE is expected to be fully operational in Spring of 2026.
