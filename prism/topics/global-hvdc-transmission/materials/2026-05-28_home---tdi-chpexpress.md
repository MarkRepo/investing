## Snippet

Developed in anticipation of New York state’s transition from fossil fuel to clean energy, the fully buried **Champlain Hudson Power Express®** transmission line is ready to power New Yorkers into the future with clean, renewable hydropower. And just in time, too, as the state’s new Climate Leadership and Community Protection Act (CLCPA) requires that New York be powered by 70 percent renewable energy by 2030. The Champlain Hudson Power Express (CHPE), a clean energy transmission project, announced the donation of $80,000 to the Montefiore Nyack Foundation to support the purchase of a new incubator for the Montefiore Nyack Hospital neonatal intensive care unit (NICU).
