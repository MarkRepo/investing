SunZia is a 550-mile 525-kV high-voltage direct-current (HVDC) transmission line project in New Mexico and Arizona, with 350 miles located within New Mexico. The SunZia transmission line will unlock the development of the SunZia wind project, a 3,500-MW wind farm with 950 turbines located in Torrance, San Miguel, and Lincoln counties in central New Mexico. The SunZia line and SunZia wind projects total $11 billion of infrastructure investment. During the construction phase, there were approximately 2,000 construction workers in the field – 500 for the transmission line and 1,500 for the wind farms.
