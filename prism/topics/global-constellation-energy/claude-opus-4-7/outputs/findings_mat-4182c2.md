## Findings — CEG FY2025 10-K

### Asset base & fleet (K1, K2 baseline)
- F1. Owned/operated nuclear capacity: ~22,000 MW across 14 stations / 25 units (US largest); Calpine acquisition adds ~25 GW gas+geothermal+solar across CAISO/ERCOT/PJM/NYISO/NEPOOL.
- F2. License expiration table: Nine Mile Point 1 and Ginna licenses expire 2029; Quad Cities 2032; Calvert Cliffs 1 / FitzPatrick 2034; Byron 2044/2046; Braidwood 2046/2047; Limerick 2044/2049; LaSalle 2042/2043; Peach Bottom 2053/2054.
- F3. ARO (decommissioning liability) FY2025 year-end: $12.908B (10-K balance sheet).
- F4. NDT (decommissioning trust) assets year-end 2025: ~$15.8B with $3.6B reduction during year from refunds and benefit payments (10-K Note 13 disclosure).

### PPA pipeline / hyperscaler demand (K1)
- F5. Microsoft Crane PPA: 20-year, ~835 MW, signed Sept 2024; ties to Crane Clean Energy Center restart targeted 2028.
- F6. Meta-Clinton PPA: 20-year, signed June 2025, commercial operation June 2027; includes ~30 MW uprate path by 2029; PPA structured to qualify Clinton uprate volumes for Section 45Y clean electricity PTC.
- F7. Co-located large-load arrangement language: 10-K confirms FERC December 2025 directive for PJM tariff revisions on co-located load; Constellation has ongoing co-location dialogues at multiple sites (referenced but no second NDA-cleared hyperscaler signed by 12/31/2025).
- F8. Existing nuclear PTC (Section 45U) revenue: $320M FY2025 vs $2,080M FY2024 (-84.6% YoY) — reflects merchant power price reset above the $44.75/MWh phase-out ceiling.
- F9. OBBBA (One Big Beautiful Bill Act, July 2025) preserved 45U through 2032 and Section 45Y for new nuclear/uprates through 2035.

### Crane restart progress (K2)
- F10. DOE Loan Programs Office $1B loan guarantee closed November 2025; matures October 2055 at T+37.5bps; supports Crane refurbishment.
- F11. NRC operating license renewal: Three Mile Island Unit 1 (Crane) license currently expires 2029; renewal application status: filed but not yet issued by NRC as of 10-K date.
- F12. Crane subsequent license renewal target: 2034 application for extension to 2054 (per fleet renewal cadence in 10-K).
- F13. 10-K capex disclosure: $5.7B 2026 / $4.7B 2027 capital plan including $3.9B growth (Crane refurb, MW uprates, co-location enabling).

### Calpine integration & financials (K3)
- F14. Calpine purchase consideration ~$22B: 50M Constellation common shares + $4.5B cash; closed 2026 Q1 (subsequent event in 10-K).
- F15. Lock-up provisions on shares issued to Calpine sellers: 50% expires 6/30/2026, 50% expires 6/30/2027.
- F16. DOJ resolution required divestiture of 6 generation assets: signed March 2026 LS Power agreement for 5 PJM gas plants (4.4 GW total) for ~$5B; ERCOT plant divestiture pending.
- F17. Acquisition / merger transaction costs FY2025: $97M (10-K Adjusted Operating Earnings reconciliation, pre-tax).

### FY2025 reported financials
- F18. Total revenues FY2025: $25,533M vs $23,568M (+8.3% YoY).
- F19. Operating income FY2025: $3,086M vs $4,317M (-28.5%); GAAP net income $2,319M vs $3,749M (-38.1%).
- F20. GAAP diluted EPS FY2025: $7.40 vs $11.89 prior; weighted average diluted shares 314M.
- F21. Adjusted (non-GAAP) Operating Earnings FY2025: $9.39 EPS vs $8.67 prior year (+8.3%).

### PJM capacity & state programs (K4 backdrop)
- F22. PJM capacity prices for 2025/26 delivery year: Mid-Atlantic East $179.79/MW-day (+247% YoY); ComEd $169.50/MW-day (+445% YoY).
- F23. PJM capacity collar for 2028/29 and 2029/30 BRA delivery years: floor $175/MW-day, cap $325/MW-day (FERC accepted April 2026; tariff change disclosed in 10-K subsequent events).
- F24. State ZEC: NY ZEC extended through 2049 (January 2026 PSC order); Illinois CMC program runs through 2027; New Jersey ZEC ended May 2025.

### Capital structure & returns (K5)
- F25. Year-end 2025 capital structure: 7% commercial paper / 31% long-term debt / 62% equity; $9.5B aggregate revolver/credit facility capacity.
- F26. Share repurchase program: $3B repurchased since 2023 inception; $593M authorization remaining at 12/31/2025; no Q4 2025 buybacks (window closed during Calpine deal).
- F27. FY2026 declared dividend: $0.4265/qtr ($1.706 annualized), +10% YoY; Calpine acquisition-related capex commitment limits near-term incremental return.

### Narrative thread (management framing)
管理层口径: Constellation 定位为同时服务 hyperscaler 负载增长的「基荷+灵活气电」一体化平台 — Microsoft 和 Meta 交易验证长久期核电 PPA 的「先发溢价」；Calpine 增加 25 GW 可调度气电以获取 PJM/ERCOT 容量稀缺性；OBBBA 保留 45U/45Y 移除了下个十年的税收抵免悬而未决问题。

### Contrarian points
- C1. FY2025 GAAP 净利同比下滑 38%，营收却 +8% — 主因核电 PTC 收入下降 84.6% + Calpine 交易成本；FY2025 GAAP 盈利质量与「成长故事」脱钩。
- C2. 核电 PTC 在更高 merchant 电价下基本失效（$320M vs $2,080M）；新增长必须来自 PPA 单价上行和 Calpine，而非 PTC 收割。
- C3. DOJ 要求剥离 6 处资产暗示 Calpine 交易的监管成本超基线预期；LS Power 收益（$5B）仅占 $22B 交易价的 ~23%。
- C4. 锁定结构（2026 年 6 月 50% / 2027 年 6 月 50%）造成两个大型潜在股票悬而未决日期；Calpine 卖方收到 5,000 万股（~14% 全摊薄股本）。
- C5. 许可证更新时点：NMP1 和 Ginna 都在 2029 年到期 — 同年是 Crane 重启目标年；监管里程碑集中在单一年度。

### K# verdicts
- K1 ✅ — Microsoft 835MW/20yr + Meta-Clinton 20yr（≥30MW uprate）跨过 ≥2 hyperscaler / ≥500MW / ≥15yr / 18 月门槛。
- K2 ❓ — DOE $1B 担保已关账（正面）；NRC 许可证更新尚未颁发；2028 重启时间表在 10-K 中重申但取决于 NRC + CIR 转让（subsequent event）。
- K3 ❓ — Calpine 刚关账；FY2025 10-K 显示 $97M 交易成本但尚无完整年度合并核电 segment EBITDA margin；未披露 FY2027 ≥55% 核电 EBITDA margin。
- K4 ❓ — PPA 定价未在 10-K 披露（商业机密）；只知道容量电价（PJM Mid-Atl $179.79/MW-day, ComEd $169.50/MW-day）和 PJM 2028/29 collar $175-325/MW-day。
- K5 ❓ — 派息 +10% 至 $0.4265/qtr 信号信心；$593M 回购授权相对规模较小；未发布 FY2028+ 数字 guidance。
