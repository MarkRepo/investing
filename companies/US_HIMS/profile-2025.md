# Hims & Hers Health, Inc. · FY2025 快照（草稿）


## 1. 业务本质

Hims & Hers is a consumer-first digital health platform combining a provider network, EMR system, digital prescriptions, cloud-enabled pharmacy fulfillment, and personalization capabilities. Primary conditions covered: sexual health, hair loss, hormone health, weight loss, dermatology, mental health, plus laboratory testing services. Revenue model is predominantly subscription-based.

## 9. 风险因素（Top 5）

1. **We operate in a highly regulated, dynamic environment and are subject to an increasing number of laws and regulations**: Exposure to telehealth, pharmacy, compounding, peptide development, and laboratory testing regulations across multiple jurisdictions; subject to federal and state enforcement actions.

2. **We operate in highly competitive markets and face competition from large, well-established healthcare providers**: Direct competition from traditional healthcare providers, telehealth platforms, retail pharmacies, pharmaceutical companies, and technology companies with superior resources and brand recognition.

3. **If we are unable to expand or maintain the scope of our offerings**: Must continually expand product and service offerings including telehealth, prescription medications, and non-prescription products; regulatory changes could necessitate modifications or restrictions to platform offerings.

4. **If we are unable to successfully market to new customers and retain existing customers**: Dependent on marketing effectiveness and cost efficiency; evolving privacy and healthcare regulations may restrict marketing activities and social media platform usage.

5. **We are dependent on our relationships with the Affiliated Medical Groups**: Relies on affiliated medical groups and independent contractor providers for healthcare delivery; relationship disruption would materially impact business and growth.
